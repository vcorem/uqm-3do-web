01 / Start here

A ship with one extra chair.

Seven years after the Sa-Matra’s defeat, the Wayfarer surveys settlements beyond the old Hierarchy frontier. An abandoned planetary shield requests a passenger berth. The caller is new, alive, and difficult to fit into a reconstruction system that already knows how every request should be answered.

Original victories remain meaningful. The new story grows from the unfinished work of liberation, with humor, material constraints and people whose interests do not neatly agree.

What the player gets

Star chart, partial resupply, complete transmissions by default with optional short passages, portraits, musical themes, evidence and quest journal, full conversation log, protected saves with backup recovery and import/export, three surface modes, equipment and hull progression, space battles and three authored endings.

Reading is untimed. Travel and certain decisions advance days. The journal exposes commitments: delay can consume reserves, require repairs or turn a free hull grant into a paid replacement. Resource exhaustion, damaged relationships, retreat and defeat retain explicit recovery paths.

Revision / Playability review

Discoveries now change the voyage.

The review found repeated surface tasks, little equipment progression, fragile saves, unearned memories and choices whose aftermath ignored the decision. This revision addresses those issues in the playable campaign.

Investigate and build

Three evidence-based investigations, useful field discoveries, six upgrades, five attainable hulls and two completed destination questlines connect exploration to capability.

Different ways to act

Mapping, recovery shuttles and relay expeditions have different physical objectives. Each political ending has its own three-stage operation, with remembered engineering and personal consequences.

Keep the voyage coherent

Announced deadlines have recovery routes; battle retries preserve rewards; crew reserves and delivered rescues persist. Optional memories, opposing decisions and old-save aftermaths now follow actual history.

The story remains a smaller, predominantly dialogue-led adventure. Shorter prose and clearer agency improve its design; independent players are still needed to judge enjoyment, emotional impact and lasting replay value.

Plot architecture — major spoilers

The reconstruction network creates provisional Afterlight minds to forecast decisions, then discards versions that refuse. Sera authorized predictive assent without understanding the lives being erased. Discovery does not make her previous authority legitimate or her relief work worthless.

The pluralist accord requires actual clocks, public witnesses, independent capacity and a prepared harbor, followed by a buffer, staged timing and a live-refusal test. The mandate isolates its control panel, tests failover and assigns the breaker authority. Partition chooses where people can go, preserves a bridge and witnesses the final separation. Each settlement has its own operation, repairable mistakes, selectable terms and conditional personal epilogues. The game does not reduce this to a universal morality score.

The story bible records the full four-act outline, faction motives, reveal timing, clue routes, canonical boundaries and unresolved mysteries.

Authored data, deterministic rules, real encounters.

Buildscripts/build-afterlight.shClean portable output, SHA-256 file manifest, no stale build assets. Refuses to replace unrelated output directories.

The campaign shell and new surface game are JavaScript. Space combat uses the existing Rust translation and preserves original UQM pixel-art ships and sound effects. This is not a claim that the entire campaign is Rust, that all ships were redrawn, or that the original lander was ported into this new interface.

Hosting

Serve .tools/afterlight-dist/ over HTTPS with Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin and Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp. Serve .mjs as JavaScript and .wasm as WebAssembly. The included Rust server already provides the necessary behavior. Localhost is sufficient for local play. No campaign server, AI subscription, telemetry or remote media service is required. For a downloadable release from a clean committed checkout, run bash scripts/package-afterlight-release.sh. The resulting ZIP includes an exact corresponding source archive, revision, file hashes and hosting instructions.

Use a dedicated path or origin for preview. The existing Original/HD launchers and multiplayer system remain separate. Deployment and a merge to main have not been requested.

Recorded limitations

  • Literary quality, emotional effect, perceived difficulty and sustained enjoyment still require independent human playthroughs.
  • The campaign is substantially smaller and more dialogue-led than Star Control II; word counts include all branches and conditional passages.
  • Mapping, recovery and relay expeditions are compact optional activities, not large action levels.
  • Recorded voices, new native ship sprite sets and localization are not included.
  • Playwright Chromium, Firefox and WebKit were checked on macOS; this is not every Safari release, physical phone, assistive technology or long-session configuration.
  • Actual purchased-ship launch/withdrawal and partial rescue were checked in each browser. The complete 25-hull native matrix and victory/defeat evidence belong to the separately recorded Chromium run; that full matrix was not rerun in Firefox/WebKit.
  • Native space combat requires a keyboard; narrow-screen reading, outfitting and surface pointer controls were checked.

06 / Continue development

A complete campaign, ready for human review.

Keep the story schema and state tests together. For any plot edit, validate references and replay affected ending and recovery routes. Native Rust hooks are documented and preserved in campaign/native-hooks.patch; apply them to a staging translation rather than overwriting the working engine.

Before a public release

Invite independent players to complete several routes without a guide. Review pacing, humor, emotional payoffs, music fatigue, difficulty and accessibility. Extend the recorded browser matrix to longer sessions, actual mobile devices and assistive technologies. Playwright WebKit evidence is not a claim of testing every Safari release or device. Mobile reading and surface controls are provided; native battles still need a keyboard.

A test suite establishes functional evidence. It cannot establish that a new story equals or exceeds Star Control II. That judgment belongs to readers and players.
