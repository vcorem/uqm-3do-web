Original universe

Star Control II was created by Paul Reiche III, Fred Ford and their collaborators. The Ur-Quan Masters is the community continuation of its released source and content. This project is an unofficial fan campaign; its new events, characters and interpretations are not official canon.

New campaign

Created for the uqm-3do-web project. Original campaign writing, implementation, synthesized compositions and production by Codex for this repository. The new portrait and scene illustrations were produced with OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool. The original musical scores are authored note sequences rendered with a reproducible software synthesizer; they contain no sampled recordings or borrowed melodies.

The campaign has English dialogue text and an instrumental score. There are no recorded voice performances. The artwork is new; the genuine UQM combat preserves the original ship artwork, mechanics and sound effects.
