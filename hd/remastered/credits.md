# Melee Remastered presentation

The Ur-Quan Masters ship designs and the original/HD reference artwork retain
their upstream authorship and notices. See `hd/content-lock.json` and the
distributed `content-licenses.txt`; this presentation does not replace those
notices or claim ownership of the ship designs.

New painted ship renderings, nebula and explosion atlas were created for this
branch with the built-in image generation tool. Generation direction and
processing are recorded in `art/provenance.json`. The source atlases are included
so the compiled visual frames can be regenerated. Free Stars: Children of
Infinity was a visual reference; no assets were taken from that game.

The two new music compositions, their scores, synthesis source and production
credits are in `music/credits.json` and `music/README.md`.

Code changes follow the existing GPL-2.0-or-later engine license. Upstream art
license conditions continue to apply to derivative ship renderings.
